## P3.1 — LST depeg breaker (pure logic)
An LST trades at a **fair value = exchange_rate × underlying** (the P2.4/P2.6
pricing). A depeg is the *market* price diverging from that fair value (the LST
discounts vs. its redemption value) — distinct from the USDC fixed-$1 peg.

**Decisions (autonomous):**
- `LstDepegBreaker(threshold_bps=200, sustain=1)` — default **2%** (mid of the
  design's 1–2% band). `sustain` = consecutive off-peg observations required to
  trip ("sustained", not a transient blip); default 1, set higher in prod.
- `update(market_price, fair_price)`: `deviation_bps = |market − fair| / fair ×
  10_000`. Off-peg (`> threshold`) increments a consecutive counter; an on-peg
  reading resets it. `is_tripped` when `consecutive ≥ sustain`.
- Fail-safe-open: untripped before the first update (no LST price feed → never
  trips), mirroring the USDC breaker.

**Tasks (TDD):** on-peg → untripped; single off-peg with sustain=1 → tripped;
off-peg with sustain=3 trips only on the 3rd consecutive; an on-peg reading
resets the counter; deviation computed against fair (not a fixed 1.0); untripped
before first update.

**File:** `decision_engine/risk/lst_depeg_breaker.py` + test. Wired into the
health monitor at P3.2 (not a standalone gate checker — the monitor decides the
de-risk; an LST depeg de-risks the affected sleeve rather than halting all
rebalancing the way a USDC depeg does).
